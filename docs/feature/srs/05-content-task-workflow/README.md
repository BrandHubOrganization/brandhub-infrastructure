## 1. Domain Architecture Overview

Domain `3.6` is the core operational production engine of BrandHub, spanning 7 tightly integrated subsystems across `brandhub-business-service`, `brandhub-api-gateway`, `brandhub-web`, and `brandhub-mobile-app`:

1. **Five-State Task Workflow, Role-Section Workspaces & Rich Comments (`3.6.1` – `3.6.3`):**
   - **Controllers:** `TaskWorkflowController`, `TaskSectionController`, `CampaignSubtaskController`, `TaskCommentController`, `TaskCatalogController`, `TaskExportController`.
   - **Architecture:** MongoDB `tasks` (`TaskDocument` with 5 canonical states: `TODO` / `BACKLOG`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `IN_REVIEW`, `FINISHED`, `CANCELLED`, optimistic `workflowRevision`, and `firstStartedAt` brief/assignee lock), role-based multi-section editors (`task_sections` & `task_section_versions` for `EVENT_PLAN`, `WORKSHOP`, `KOL`, `PRESS_RECOMMENDATION`, `NEWSLETTER`, `SURVEY`), Jira-style subtasks, and rich `@mention` + image-attachment comments (`task_comments` + `task_notification_outbox`).
2. **Multi-View Task Board, Calendar, Gantt Timeline & Unified Filtering (`3.6.4` – `3.6.8`):**
   - **Controllers:** `TaskController.getTasks` (`GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/tasks`), `CampaignTimelineController` (`/campaigns/{campaignId}/timeline`).
   - **Architecture:** Single server-filtered, Workspace-isolated MongoDB query (`status`, `type`, `search`, `campaignId`, `from`, `to`, `assignee`, `due`, stable `_id` tie-break) shared across List, Calendar, Gantt Timeline, and Kanban Board views with zero mock-data fallbacks.
3. **Sequential Multi-Round Approval & Collaborative Yjs Content Writing (`3.6.9` – `3.6.10`, `3.6.33` – `3.6.34`):**
   - **Controllers:** `TaskApprovalController`, `TaskPostController`, `TaskController` (`/content`, `/content/versions`), `TaskContentWebSocketController` (`/tasks/{taskId}/content` Yjs CRDT + awareness), `AiCaptionController` (`/captions/moderate`).
   - **Architecture:** Enforces atomic CAS transitions (`Creator` submit/withdraw -> `Manager` approve/reject -> optional `Client` approve/reject) over MongoDB `tasks`, `posts` (`task_id` partial unique), and `task_approvals`, paired with Tiptap + Yjs collaborative editing (`task_content_operations` & `task_content_snapshots` in PostgreSQL), 36-style Unicode formatting, pre-publish AI compliance moderation (`BR-42`), and zero-credit version history restoration (`BR-35`).
4. **Encrypted S3 Material Repository, Nested Folders, Client Vault, Google Drive Import & Watermarking (`3.6.11` – `3.6.19`):**
   - **Controllers:** `MaterialController`, `MaterialFolderController`, `MaterialVersionController`, `RepositoryEncryptedMediaController`, `ClientVaultController`, `CustomerDriveController`, `DriveFolderImportController`, `WatermarkPresetController`.
   - **Architecture:** AWS S3 primary storage (`SYSTEM_S3`, 5 GB free baseline per `Owner`/`Client` via `StorageQuotaService` + monthly add-ons) protected by pre-S3 envelope encryption (`RepositoryEnvelopeCrypto` + capability URLs `/api/v1/repository-media`, blocking `Admin`/Dev plaintext access), nested folder trees (`material_folders`), SHA-256 duplicate resolution & file versioning (`material_versions` `v1..vN`), `RAW` unedited photo warnings, `Client` Personal Vault cross-workspace folder sharing (`client_vault_shares`), one-time Google Drive folder tree import to S3 (`DriveFolderImportService`), 30-day soft-delete trash + manual/bulk purge, and Luma-adaptive single/tiled brand watermarking (`ImageWatermarkProcessor`).
5. **Workspace Hashtag Collection & Contextual AI Suggestions (`3.6.20` – `3.6.24`):**
   - **Controllers:** `HashtagController` (`/api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/hashtags/*`).
   - **Architecture:** PostgreSQL `hashtags` (`BRAND`, `CAMPAIGN`, `TRENDING`, `EVENT_SPECIFIC`) with normalized uniqueness, editor one-click insertion, and AI caption/platform contextual hashtag generation.
6. **Public/Task Surveys with Google Sheets Sync & Online Meeting Links (`3.6.25` – `3.6.27`):**
   - **Controllers:** `SurveyController`, `PublicSurveyController` (`/api/v1/public/surveys/{token}`), `WorkspaceSurveyController`, and `/api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/tasks/{taskId}/meeting-link`.
   - **Architecture:** MongoDB `surveys` & `survey_responses` with live Google Sheets creation/append (`GoogleSheetsService`), rating/choice/text analytics (`/survey/analysis`), and instant Google Meet link provisioning for online Workshop/coordination tasks.
7. **Block-Based Mail Templates, Batch Delivery & Multi-Channel Realtime Chat + Voice Calls (`3.6.28` – `3.6.32`, `3.6.35`):**
   - **Controllers:** `MailTemplateController`, `AgencyMailTemplateController`, `MailAttachmentController`, `MailRecipientController`, `MailSendController`, `MailBatchController`, `MailSuppressionController`, `PublicMailController`, plus `ChatController`, `ChatMessageController`, `ChatChannelController`, `ChatChannelMessageController`, `ChatAttachmentController`, `ChatScheduleController`, `ChatPreferenceController`, `ChatCallController`.
   - **Architecture:** Drag-and-drop block email builder (`MailBlockRenderer`), quota-governed async batch delivery (`MailDeliveryWorker`), RFC-compliant unsubscribe/suppression list, and STOMP WebSocket `/ws/chat` collaboration (`DIRECT`/`PUBLIC`/`PRIVATE` channels, threads, reactions, pins, `@mentions` inbox, `/task` & `/material` slash cards, 10 MiB / repository pinned attachments, scheduled messages, DND/mute preferences, and Agora RTC voice calls).
